I used to be stuck in my past regrets.

Countless times, I imagined going back in time. I wondered what changes I would make to fix my mistakes and avoid those hurts.

Then I had my son. Watching him grow, from the day he was born, through learning to speak and walk, to being able to talk with me, I have truly felt how much he relies on me, cares for me, and loves me. As I love him, I am loved in return, softly healed by his love, pulled out of the past I couldn't let go.

Now, even with those regrets still there, and even though I'm past forty, I would never trade this for twenty or thirty years of my youth. I am far too afraid that changing the past might mean never having my son. Being his dad is already the greatest blessing of my life, and that is more than enough. I love him so much, my precious boy.
